Misir Wot

Misir Wot is a traditional Ethiopian spicy red lentil stew made with aromatic spices, onions, and berbere.

ingredients

  • Red lentils 1 cup rinsed
  • Neutral oil 3 to 4 tablespoons
  • Onion 1 large finely diced
  • Garlic 3 cloves minced
  • Ginger 1 teaspoon, freshly grated (optional)
  • Berbere spice blend: 2 to 3 tablespoons (adjust to your spice level)
  • Tomato paste 2 tablespoons
  • Water 2.5 to 3 cups
  • Salt 1 teaspoon or to taste

steps

  1. Stir the onions: Heat a pot over medium heat. Add the diced onions and dry-stir them for 5–8 minutes until soft and translucent (add a splash of water if they stick).
  2. Add oil, minced garlic, and ginger. Cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Build the flavor base: Add the tomato paste. Stir continuously for 2–3 minutes to cook out the raw tomato flavor and bloom the spices.
  4. Simmer the lentils: Add the rinsed red lentils and water. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.
